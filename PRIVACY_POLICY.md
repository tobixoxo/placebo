# Privacy Policy

Placebo is designed with user privacy in mind. This app does not collect, transmit, or store personal information outside the user's device.

## What data is stored

- All streaks, completions, reminder settings, and app preferences are stored locally on the device using the Room database.
- The app does not require internet access and does not upload any user data.
- There is no login, sign-up, or cloud synchronization feature.

## Permissions used

- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` is used only to show reminder notifications to the user.
- `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` is used only to schedule reliable streak reminder alarms.
- `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` is used only to reschedule active reminders after device restart.

## No third-party tracking

- The app does not include analytics SDKs, tracking services, or advertising.
- The app does not share data with third parties.

## Contact

If you have questions about privacy or data handling, please contact the developer.
